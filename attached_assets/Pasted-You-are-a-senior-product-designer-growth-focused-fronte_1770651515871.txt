You are a senior product designer + growth-focused frontend engineer working on a consumer fintech app called Lifestyle Creep.

The signup screen already works functionally (Apple, Google, Email auth).
Your task is to polish, elevate, and optimize this screen for clarity, trust, and conversion without changing core auth logic.

Context
	•	App theme: minimal, dark, premium, calm, judgment-free
	•	Core promise: “2 minutes a day to master your money decisions”
	•	Audience: smart professionals who overspend subtly (subscriptions, lifestyle inflation, impulse upgrades)
	•	Emotional tone: supportive, non-shaming, quietly confident

Your goals
	1.	Improve first-impression clarity (what is this? why should I care?)
	2.	Increase signup conversion
	3.	Reduce decision anxiety
	4.	Make the app feel safe, private, and intelligent

⸻

Tasks

1. Headline & Subheadline
	•	Propose 3 improved headline + subheadline pairs
	•	Each option should target a different angle:
	•	Behavioral psychology
	•	Gamification / streaks
	•	Long-term wealth identity

2. Microcopy Improvements
Rewrite or enhance:
	•	Button labels (Apple / Google / Email)
	•	“No spam. No judgment.” line
	•	Privacy / social proof language

Make microcopy:
	•	Short
	•	Human
	•	Reassuring
	•	Slightly clever, not try-hard

3. Trust & Social Proof
Add lightweight trust signals that don’t feel salesy, such as:
	•	Subtle social proof
	•	Privacy reassurance
	•	“Used by people like you” framing

Provide exact copy suggestions.

4. Visual / Layout Tweaks (No New Screens)
Suggest specific UI refinements, such as:
	•	Spacing, hierarchy, contrast
	•	Where to place reassurance text
	•	How to visually reduce friction
	•	What should be more prominent vs quieter

Do NOT add new flows or screens.

5. Optional Delight (If Appropriate)
Suggest one small moment of delight (copy, animation, or interaction) that reinforces the brand without hurting seriousness.

⸻

Constraints
	•	Keep it minimalist
	•	Do NOT add paywalls or pricing
	•	Do NOT add long explanations
	•	Assume mobile-first
	•	Assume dark mode is default

⸻

Output Format
	•	Clear sections
	•	Bullet points
	•	Exact copy where applicable
	•	Actionable UI suggestions (not vague advice)

Think like someone trying to make this app feel like “Calm meets Duolingo for money decisions.”
